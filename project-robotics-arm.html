Robotic Arm Design & Simulation

A two-link (2R) planar robotic arm: from CAD assembly to kinematics and closed-loop motion control.

DOF
  • 2 (2R Planar)
Design
  • PTC Creo
Simulation
  • Simulink
  • Simscape Multibody
Control
  • PID

Designed and assembled a two-link (2R) planar robotic arm in PTC Creo, stepping outside my usual SolidWorks workflow as a personal challenge. The CAD assembly (base link, arm 1, arm 2) was imported into MATLAB/Simulink through Simscape Multibody, converting the Creo geometry into a physically simulated multibody model with accurate mass and inertia properties carried over from the CAD. On top of that model, I implemented forward and inverse kinematics to relate joint angles to end-effector position, generated joint trajectories, and used PID controllers to drive and simulate the arm's motion toward target poses. It's a compact project, but it connected CAD design, kinematics, and control in a single working pipeline, end to end, from a solid model to a controlled simulation.

Key methods

Forward & inverse kinematics Joint trajectory generation PID motion control CAD → Simscape Multibody import
